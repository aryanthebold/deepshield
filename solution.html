Our Solution

Three modules.
One shield.

DeepShield is India's first free, multi-modal AI forensics platform — detecting synthetic images, cloned voices, and manipulated videos in real-time. No technical knowledge needed.

Detection Modules

Built around the three attack surfaces

🖼
Module 01
Image Forensics
Upload any image — photo, screenshot, meme, or profile picture — and DeepShield runs it through Sightengine's AI API combined with local forensic rules to detect GAN artifacts, unnatural skin smoothing, metadata anomalies, and pixel-level inconsistencies.
TECH: Sightengine API · ELA Analysis · EXIF Forensics · GAN Artifact Detection
96% accuracy on AI-generated faces
🎬
Module 02
Video Analysis
Frame-by-frame temporal analysis on MP4, AVI, and MOV files. Our system samples key frames, runs each through the image module, then aggregates scores to surface a confidence metric and a suspicious-frames chart highlighting exact manipulation timestamps.
TECH: OpenCV Frame Sampling · Temporal Consistency Scoring · Anomaly Charting
Pinpoints exact manipulated frames
🎙
Module 03
Voice Detection
A 7-signal acoustic analysis pipeline — MFCC fingerprinting, pitch variance, spectral flatness, formant dynamics, breathing pattern detection, ZCR regularity, and high-frequency energy analysis — fused into a single authenticity verdict with plain-language explanation.
TECH: Librosa · MFCC · ML Ensemble (RF + GBM) · 7-Rule Forensic Pipeline
Detects ElevenLabs-style voice clones
Design Principles

Built on four pillars

⚡
Real-Time Detection
Results arrive in under 30 seconds — fast enough to verify suspicious media before you forward it, share it, or act on it. No waiting, no queues. Unlike enterprise tools that take minutes, DeepShield is built for instant verification.
🌐
Zero Barrier Access
Works on any smartphone browser and through WhatsApp and Telegram — the two platforms 500M+ Indians already use daily. No app download, no account creation, no technical knowledge required. Just forward and scan.
🧠
Explainable AI
Unlike black-box detectors, DeepShield tells you *why* it flagged something. Every verdict comes with a signal breakdown: which acoustic markers were abnormal, which visual artifacts triggered the alert, and a plain-language summary in Hindi and English.
🔒
100% Free for Citizens
DeepShield is built for public good, not profit. The web platform, WhatsApp bot, and Telegram bot are all completely free. No paywalls, no premium tiers for citizens. Built under SDG 16 to enable peace, justice, and strong institutions.
