Attack Vectors

How scammers are exploiting AI

Vector 01 · Voice Cloning
Fake Family Emergency Calls
Scammers clone a relative's voice from a 3-second WhatsApp audio message, then call elderly parents claiming a medical emergency and demanding immediate UPI transfers.
↑ 340% increase in 2024 alone
Vector 02 · Synthetic Faces
KYC & Identity Fraud
AI-generated faces pass liveness checks on banking apps. GAN-forged Aadhaar selfies are used to open fake accounts for money laundering and loan fraud.
↑ 200% rise in KYC fraud cases
Vector 03 · Manipulated Video
Celebrity Scam Promotions
Deepfake videos of politicians, cricketers, and Bollywood stars promoting fraudulent investment schemes go viral on WhatsApp and Telegram, draining victims' savings.
↑ ₹500Cr+ lost to video scams in FY24
Vector 04 · Synthetic Audio
Fake Officer Impersonation
AI clones the voices of RBI officials, police officers, and CBI agents to coerce victims into transferring money under the guise of "digital arrest" or account freezes.
↑ Most reported scam type, MHA 2025
Real-World Cases

It's already happening

February 2024 · Hyderabad
IT Engineer Loses Life Savings to Voice Clone
A software engineer received a call from what sounded exactly like his father, claiming he'd been arrested in Delhi. The voice was cloned from a 10-second audio clip shared in a family WhatsApp group. He transferred ₹2.7 lakh before realising the call was fake.
LOSS: ₹2,70,000
August 2024 · Lucknow
Retired Teacher Targeted by Deepfake Video
A 67-year-old retiree received a WhatsApp video of PM Modi announcing a government pension scheme. The video was a deepfake. She invested her entire retirement corpus of ₹4 lakh into the fraudulent link embedded in the message.
LOSS: ₹4,00,000
November 2024 · Mumbai
Corporate Executive Falls for AI-Powered CEO Fraud
A finance manager received a video call from his company's CEO — or what appeared to be one. The AI-generated video was flawless. He authorised a wire transfer of ₹18 lakh to a fraudulent vendor account before the real CEO walked into the office.
LOSS: ₹18,00,000
January 2025 · Rajasthan
Rural Farmer Victim of Synthetic Land Officer
A farmer was called by an AI-cloned "tehsildar" voice demanding a bribe to process land registration papers. Without the means to verify, he paid ₹40,000 in cash and received nothing in return.
LOSS: ₹40,000
